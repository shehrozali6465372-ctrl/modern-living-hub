Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including name, email address, and account credentials when you register for our services. We also automatically collect certain information when you visit our website, including IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, and usage patterns.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience, analyze website traffic, and personalize content. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. For more details, see our Cookie Policy.

Analytics

We use analytics tools to understand how visitors interact with our website. These tools may collect anonymized data about page views, session duration, and navigation patterns. This data helps us improve our platform and user experience.

API Usage

When you use our API services, we may collect information about your API requests, including endpoints accessed, request volume, and performance metrics. This data is used to maintain service quality, detect abuse, and improve our API offerings.

OAuth Authentication

We use OAuth 2.0 for secure authentication when connecting third-party services. We never store your passwords for connected services. OAuth tokens are encrypted and stored securely, used only to facilitate authorized API requests on your behalf.

Pinterest API

When you connect your Pinterest account to Modern Living Hub, we access Pinterest API data only with your explicit permission. This includes read access to boards and pins, and write access for scheduled pin creation. We comply with Pinterest's API Terms of Service and never share your Pinterest data without consent.

Google API

If you connect Google services, we access only the data necessary for the functionality you request. We comply with Google's API Terms of Service and Limited Use requirements. Your Google data is never used for unrelated purposes.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256), regular security audits, and strict access controls. Our infrastructure is hosted on secure, SOC 2-compliant cloud providers.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data. You may also object to or restrict certain processing activities. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@modernlivinghub.com. We respond to all legitimate requests within 30 days.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@modernlivinghub.com or through our contact page.
