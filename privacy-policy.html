Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Overview

Modern Living Hub is a multi-platform content publishing tool that currently integrates with Pinterest and TikTok via their official APIs. This Privacy Policy explains what data is accessed, how it is used, and how it is protected when you use the Platform.

Modern Living Hub does not provide a traditional user registration or account system. You do not create a Modern Living Hub account, and we do not collect or store a password from you. Your access to the Platform is browser-based and does not require account credentials.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide the Platform's third-party integrations and to keep the service running:

  • Pinterest profile data — When you connect Pinterest via OAuth, we receive your Pinterest user ID, username, and profile image through the Pinterest API, solely to confirm the connection and display status in the UI.
  • TikTok creator data — When you connect TikTok via OAuth, we receive your TikTok open ID, creator username, creator nickname, available privacy settings, and posting permissions through the TikTok API, solely to support posting functionality you request.
  • Third-party platform tokens — Pinterest access/refresh tokens and TikTok access/refresh tokens are obtained during OAuth and stored server-side, encrypted, to maintain the authorized connection you established.
  • Content you submit — Images, videos, captions, board selections, privacy settings, and other metadata you explicitly choose to publish are sent to the relevant platform's API at your request.
  • Session and handoff data — Ephemeral session identifiers and one-time handoff codes used to securely complete OAuth across our GitHub Pages frontend and Render backend.
  • Server logs — Basic operational data (request timestamps, IP addresses, browser user-agent strings) logged automatically by our hosting providers (GitHub Pages, Render) for security and operational purposes.

Cookies and Session Data

The Platform uses encrypted server-side session cookies to maintain your third-party OAuth connections across page loads. These cookies contain only opaque session identifiers — they never contain your Pinterest or TikTok credentials, access tokens, or personal passwords. You can clear sessions at any time by disconnecting a third-party integration. For more detail, see our Cookie Policy.

How We Use Your Data

The data described above is used strictly to:

  • Complete OAuth authorization flows for Pinterest and TikTok when you initiate them.
  • Maintain your authorized connections to Pinterest and TikTok so the Platform can perform actions you request.
  • Send content, publish pins or videos, create boards, and retrieve status updates on your behalf through the respective platform APIs.
  • Operate, secure, and debug the Platform's backend services.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Pinterest Integration

When you connect your Pinterest account to Modern Living Hub, we access Pinterest data only with your explicit OAuth consent. We request the following Pinterest API scopes:

  • boards:read and boards:write — To list your boards and let you select or create a board for pin publishing.
  • pins:read and pins:write — To publish pins and check pin status on your behalf.

Pinterest access and refresh tokens are stored server-side, encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and are never exposed to the browser, any public page, or logs. We comply with Pinterest's API Terms of Service. You may disconnect Pinterest at any time, which immediately invalidates the stored tokens.

TikTok Integration

When you connect your TikTok account to Modern Living Hub, we access TikTok data only with your explicit OAuth consent. We request the following TikTok API scope:

  • video.publish — To initialize, upload, and publish videos on your behalf.

Through this authorization we also receive your TikTok creator profile (open ID, username, nickname, privacy options, and posting permissions) to display your connection status and support the posting workflow.

TikTok access and refresh tokens are stored server-side, encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and are never exposed to the browser, any public page, or logs. We comply with TikTok's Developer Terms and Content Posting API policies. You may disconnect TikTok at any time, which immediately invalidates the stored tokens.

Modern Living Hub never posts to TikTok without your explicit action — you must select content, enter a caption, choose privacy settings, and confirm each post individually.

OAuth and Third-Party Authorization

Modern Living Hub uses OAuth 2.0 to authorize connections to Pinterest and TikTok. We never see, collect, or store your Pinterest or TikTok passwords. OAuth authorization is handled entirely through the official Pinterest and TikTok authorization pages. Once you approve access, the third-party platform issues an access token to our server, which we store encrypted to maintain your connection.

Pinterest and TikTok are independent third-party services with their own privacy policies and terms of service. Modern Living Hub is not responsible for the privacy practices of these platforms.

Data Security

We use the following measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption in transit — All communication with the Platform, GitHub Pages, Render, and the Pinterest/TikTok APIs occurs over HTTPS (TLS).
  • Encryption at rest — Third-party platform tokens are encrypted using AES-256-GCM with a server-side secret before storage.
  • Server-side secrets — OAuth client secrets, session secrets, and platform tokens are stored exclusively in server-side environment variables. They are never placed in frontend code, HTML, JavaScript, logs, or any publicly accessible file.
  • One-time handoff codes — OAuth session handoff between our frontend and backend uses short-lived, single-use tokens that cannot be replayed.
  • Restricted CORS — Backend API access is restricted to the official Modern Living Hub origin only.

Hosting Providers

GitHub Pages — The frontend website is hosted on GitHub Pages, provided by GitHub, Inc. GitHub Pages may collect basic server log information including IP addresses, request timestamps, and browser user-agent strings. For more information, see GitHub's Privacy Statement at https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement.

Render — The backend API service is hosted on Render. Render may collect operational server logs including request paths, response codes, timestamps, and IP addresses for service operation and security. For more information, see Render's Privacy Policy at https://render.com/privacy.

Data Retention

Third-party platform tokens and session data are retained only for as long as your OAuth connection remains active. When you disconnect a third-party integration, all associated tokens and session data are immediately deleted server-side. Server logs are retained by our hosting providers in accordance with their standard policies.
